# University Face-Recognition App for Attendance & Access Control

A fully integrated intelligent system for **managing and recording student attendance and controlling entry to teaching and exam halls** using real-time AI face recognition — addressing the slowness, low accuracy, and manipulation risks of traditional methods.

The system guarantees that only authorized students enter halls according to predefined groups, and controls exam-hall entry against official name lists containing names, university IDs, and hall assignments.

## Technical Environment

- Python
- DeepFace
- FaceNet512
- Real-Time Face Recognition
- Anti-Spoofing
- Central Database
- Multi-Camera Support

## Core capabilities

- **Deep feature extraction:** deep-learning embeddings matched against a central database for instant identity verification.
- **Fraud and spoofing detection:** motion and texture analysis to prevent cheating, especially in sensitive exam settings.
- **Multi-camera operation:** simultaneous live streams from several cameras.
- **Automated smart reports:** daily reports, historical logs, and an analytics dashboard.

## Academic administration

Full management of departments, academic years, courses, halls, student data, recognition photos, course-student binding, and lecture session setup — with academic-administrator, secretariat, and staff roles.

## Results

Practical tests showed high recognition accuracy and response speed, with a real ability to strengthen educational security and curb manipulation, and future potential for integration with broader academic systems.
