# Automating Inquiries, Complaints & Utility Bills for Government Departments

A fully integrated service-oriented web platform that converts a set of **traditional paper transactions in government departments into an automated digital system**, focusing on three pillars: electronic complaint submission, public inquiry processing (family records and employment-status certificates), and service bill presentation and payment.

The platform offers two interfaces: a citizen portal for submitting and digitally tracking requests without paperwork, and an administrative dashboard letting government entities process requests efficiently and update their statuses (pending, completed, rejected).

## Technical Environment

- Web Development
- Administrative Information Systems
- Dashboards
- Request Status Management
- e-Government Services

## Automated services

- **Electronic complaints:** digital submission with live status tracking.
- **Public inquiries:** family records and employment certificates without office visits.
- **Utility bills:** viewing and paying bills electronically.
- **Central administration:** a request-processing dashboard with status updates and decision records.

## Administrative impact

The automation aims to improve public-service quality, minimize human error, and accelerate administrative performance — cutting time waste and making services safely available around the clock, narrowing the gap between government and citizen.

## Methodology

Implementation followed requirements analysis, then software development, then testing and evaluation to ensure a flexible, extensible architecture — with a deliberately simple, fast interface suited to the available internet speeds.
