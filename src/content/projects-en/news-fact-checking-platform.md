# Official News Platform for Combating Fake News

## Project Summary

Computer engineering students developed the **Official News Platform for Combating Fake News** as a digital system for managing news and reports related to questionable content. The system focuses on creating an organized pipeline that starts with a user submitting a report about external content, continues with the report's review by an editor, and ends with publishing a correction or confirmation and linking the content under review to the published outcome.

**Techno Enjaz contributed to supporting and guiding the project's development alongside the students**, within a framework of developing a web platform for managing content, reports, and user permissions.

The current verification mechanism relies on human review through editors; the implemented version does not include an AI-based automatic fake news detection tool.

## Project Scope

The system covered developing a structure that allows:

- Publishing approved news and content.
- Receiving user reports about questionable content.
- Reviewing reports through the system's editors.
- Creating correction or clarification posts.
- Linking the content under review to its related correction.
- Managing users and permissions.
- Organizing content by regions and categories.

---

## How the System Works

1. The user submits a report about external content that includes a link, text, or image.
2. The report arrives at the editor's dashboard for review.
3. The editor analyzes the report and takes the appropriate action.
4. A clarification or correction post is published when needed.
5. The user can access the verified information through the platform.
