# Sports Injury Detection via Image & Video Analysis

An intelligent system that analyzes **resistance training and detects movement errors** that could lead to musculoskeletal injuries, using AI and computer vision — an affordable, objective tool helping athletes evaluate their performance without direct professional supervision.

The approach converts video into a numerical representation of motion, then analyzes it temporally to reach an accurate judgment of exercise correctness, rather than relying on isolated frames.

## Technical Environment

- Artificial Intelligence
- Human Pose Estimation
- LSTM
- Computer Vision
- Motion Analysis
- Deep Learning

## Processing pipeline

- **Skeletal extraction:** human pose estimation from video frames.
- **Numerical motion representation:** joint keypoints converted into analyzable time series.
- **Temporal model:** an LSTM network analyzes the full motion sequence.
- **Performance verdict:** classifying form as correct/incorrect with near-real-time responsiveness.

## Results

The implementation demonstrated the system's ability to distinguish correct from incorrect form in the selected exercises under appropriate recording conditions, with near-real-time response — highlighting deep learning's potential for supporting sports safety and early detection of movement deviations.

## Future work

The system can be extended to more exercises, made more robust under home lighting and varying camera angles, and integrated into a mobile app delivering instant feedback to trainees.
