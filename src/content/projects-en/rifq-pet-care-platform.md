# Rifq — Smart Pet Care & Protection Platform

**Rifq** is a fully integrated Arabic web platform specialized in smart pet care, designed to serve community needs in animal protection and welfare, combining electronic shelter management, a documented adoption system, and generative AI technologies.

Instead of IoT hardware, each animal is linked to a **unique QR code** acting as a secure cloud identifier, giving users and veterinarians instant access to the full profile — medical records and behavioral assessments — with a simple phone scan.

## Technical Environment

- Laravel 12
- Filament
- Google Gemini
- QR Codes
- Online Adoption System
- E-commerce Store
- Loyalty Points

## Core services

- **AI clinic:** analyzing images and videos to diagnose skin conditions and assess psychological and behavioral state.
- **Behavior-expert chat:** instant consultations with an AI-backed animal behavior expert.
- **Online adoption:** a complete adoption request system with status tracking.
- **E-commerce store:** pet supplies with an advanced cart and a loyalty-points program.
- **Comprehensive admin panel:** fine-grained permissions for admins, veterinarians, and staff.

## Technical architecture

The platform is built with Laravel 12 and the Filament admin panel, integrating the Google Gemini API for generative diagnostic and consultation services.

## Impact

Rifq promotes animal welfare values, facilitates safe adoptions, improves veterinary care quality through AI, and builds a trusted digital bridge between citizens and animal-care organizations.
