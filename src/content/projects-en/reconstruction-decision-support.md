# AI Decision-Support System for Reconstruction and Damage Documentation

A project designing and developing an **intelligent system for documenting and analyzing damage in affected areas**, built on smartphone applications and multimodal artificial intelligence to support humanitarian planning, reconstruction, and evidence-based decision-making.

The system is based on an Android app used by volunteers and field staff to collect geotagged photographs with detailed textual descriptions; the geographic location is extracted automatically from the image metadata itself, guaranteeing spatial consistency without a separate location field.

## Technical Environment

- Android
- Laravel
- Gemini (multimodal model)
- AI Agent
- Geotagging
- Analytical Dashboard

## Intelligent data pipeline

- **Field collection:** a mobile app documenting damage with geotagged photos and detailed descriptions.
- **Central server:** a Laravel backend receiving data and feeding an intelligent processing pipeline.
- **AI agent:** a multimodal Gemini model unifying heterogeneous region names and analyzing textual and visual content.
- **Damage estimation:** a unified, statistically analyzable classification of damage level.

## Analytical dashboard

Final results are presented in an interactive dashboard letting decision-makers review heat maps and analytical charts of damage distribution and severity across districts and governorates, reducing human error and improving field-data quality.

## Impact

The system transforms damage documentation from paper reports and human estimates into intelligent digital documentation, supporting evidence-based decisions in conflict and reconstruction environments through the integration of geospatial technologies, AI, and modern information systems.
