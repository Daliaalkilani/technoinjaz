# Intelligent Military Robot with AI Target Detection

A project to design and build an **intelligent field robot** that relies on artificial intelligence technologies to improve the efficiency of field operations and reduce human risk. The robot is controlled remotely through a dedicated application and is equipped with an advanced camera and an automated turret that operate automatically using image-processing algorithms.

## Project Idea and Objectives

The project is built around a robotic platform capable of navigating and exploring complex environments, with the ability to identify and classify targets with high accuracy using the **YOLO** algorithm. This enables instant decisions without direct human intervention in hazardous locations.

The project's main objectives include:

- Remote control of the robot's movement through a dedicated application.
- Automatic target identification using computer vision and the YOLO algorithm.
- Reducing people's exposure to risk during field missions.
- Integrating wireless communications to transmit and analyze data in real time.

## How Does the System Work?

The robot uses a camera to capture the scene ahead, then processes the images in real time with image-processing algorithms to detect and classify targets. When a target is detected, the system responds automatically according to the gestures predefined by the operator.

The system integrates multiple communication technologies such as **Bluetooth** and **Wi-Fi** to facilitate control and the transfer of data and video between the robot and the control device in real time.

## Technical Environment

- **Python** for image processing and running the YOLO model.
- **YOLO** for accurate target detection and classification.
- **Bluetooth and Wi-Fi** for wireless control and data transmission.
- An embedded control platform to manage movement and the actuator units.

## Project Results

The experiment demonstrated the system's ability to identify and classify targets reliably within the test environment, with near-real-time response in control and video transmission. The project represents a practical step toward developing intelligent robotic systems that support field missions in complex environments.

## Development Opportunities

The project can be developed further by improving detection accuracy under difficult lighting conditions, supporting longer control ranges through stronger communication modules, adding autonomous navigation modes, and integrating additional sensors to increase the robot's awareness of its surroundings.
