# Engineering Project Management Platform for Investment Companies

A centralized web platform for **automating and organizing the management of engineering projects funded by foreign investments**, serving as a digital intermediary that guarantees transparency among foreign investors, local administration, and field teams — reducing communication gaps and improving decision-making efficiency.

The system uses a "technical workshops" structure to divide projects and distribute tasks, providing role-based interactive dashboards for managers, investors, supervisors, and workers.

## Technical Environment

- Laravel
- Filament
- Livewire
- Flutter
- Dart
- Explainable AI
- Bootstrap
- HTML/CSS

## Core functions

- **Smart recruitment:** AI-based resume evaluation against defined criteria, with Explainable AI support providing clear justifications for evaluation outcomes.
- **Field mobile app:** a Flutter app for engineers and workers to track daily tasks and update work status from the job site with live data flow.
- **Technical and financial reports:** continuous progress and resource tracking.
- **Real-time investor monitoring:** eliminating the "black box" in investment.

## Technical architecture

The system is built with Laravel plus Filament and Livewire for interactive development, with HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap for the UI, and the mobile app developed in Flutter and Dart.

## Impact

The platform integrates HR management with project management in a unified digital environment, boosting operational efficiency, transparency, and trust in hiring and field-execution decisions.
