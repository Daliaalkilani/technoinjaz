# Nabd — AI Psychological Assessment Support for Children

**Nabd** is an intelligent system supporting the preliminary psychological assessment of children affected by war, analyzing non-verbal behavior and vocal characteristics with AI to give psychologists objective indicators of the child's psychological state.

The system is explicitly a **decision-support tool, not a replacement for clinical diagnosis** — reducing reliance on subjective visual judgment alone and providing a documented basis for assessment decisions.

## Technical Environment

- Google Gemini
- Voice Analysis
- Non-verbal Behavior Analysis
- Multimodal AI
- Session & Report Management

## Analysis mechanism

- **Voice analysis:** extracting vocal characteristics indicative of emotional state.
- **Non-verbal behavior analysis:** tracking psychologically relevant behavioral indicators.
- **Intelligent processing:** the Google Gemini model analyzes the multimodal data.
- **Preliminary reports:** indicators that help the specialist evaluate the case and decide on next steps.

## Session management

The system provides an integrated environment for managing users, children's files, assessment sessions, and the reports produced by each analysis, documenting each child's psychological follow-up path.

## Impact

Nabd supports psychological work in crisis and war contexts by reducing subjectivity in preliminary assessment and accelerating indicator collection, while keeping the specialist as the decision-maker.
