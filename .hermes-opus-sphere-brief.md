# Fix: team sphere — duplicate face next to settled member

## Bug report from the site owner (tested on her phone/desktop)
On the 3D team sphere (InfiniteMenu), when a member settles at his seat (e.g. عبد الهادي),
his face still appears ON A NEIGHBOURING instance near him — duplicated beside himself.
A previous fix tried to stop this but didn't work in practice.

## File
`src/components/effects/InfiniteMenu.jsx` — WebGL2 shader mapping + animation logic.

## Current (broken) approach
- Fragment shader (`discFragShaderSource`) has `uFocusActive/uFocusDir/uFocusItem` uniforms:
  instances whose `dot(normalize(vWorldDir), normalize(uFocusDir)) > 0.35` AND whose
  `vInstanceId % uItemCount == uFocusItem` get remapped to another member.
- JS sets focus in `#animate` from `targetVertexIndex/targetMemberIndex` when dwelling.

## Why it likely fails
The condition `vInstanceId % uItemCount == uFocusItem` only catches instances whose STATIC
assignment equals the focus member. But with N members and more instances than members, MANY
instances around the focus can show other members — fine — yet the visible duplicate comes from
instances near the focus whose static index happens to equal focus item BUT whose world position
during dwell is not within the dot>0.35 cone (cone too narrow / focusDir wrong axis), or from the
mapping being instance-ID based while the vertex-to-image mapping after rotations differs.
Investigate properly: verify vWorldDir is the INSTANCE CENTER direction (normalize(centerPos)),
verify focusDir uses the same convention, and widen/remove the cone test.

## Required behavior (owner's words)
- The settled member is at the center: his face ONLY on his own instance.
- NO instance in the visible area around him may repeat his face. Ever. During dwell.
- Other members' faces around him are fine (varied, not all the same alternate).
- During drag/transition, normal mapping.

## Constraints
- Don't break: touch-drag scroll fix, fallback (non-WebGL) list, auto-tour, performance.
- `npm run build` must pass (run it). Do NOT deploy or commit — leave changes in the working tree.

## Verify before finishing
Reason through the math concretely: pick itemCount=12, instanceCount=~42 (whatever icoGeo gives),
focus instance v; list which instances pass your condition and prove none of them is visible near
v during dwell. Then run the build.
